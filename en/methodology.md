# Methodology

Nutripetia separates **scientific research**, **independent scientific review** and **public communication**.

We define specific research questions, evaluate the quality and limitations of sources, document uncertainty and preserve traceable records of decisions and revisions.

Our AI Canon and five scientific protocols govern the research workflow. Their detailed public description will follow verification of the official versions and publication scope.

Scientific conclusions must not be changed to serve search rankings or promotional objectives.
