# Methodology

**Research before conclusions** is Nutripetia's guiding principle.

We define research questions, identify and assess sources, evaluate potential bias, separate findings from interpretations and document uncertainty and limitations. Scientific conclusions must be reviewed before publication.

## Our commitments

- Apply consistent evidential standards to favourable and unfavourable findings.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, interpretations and unknowns.
- Preserve traceability of sources, decisions and revisions.
- Treat comparisons across species cautiously.
- Keep the evaluation of the research system separate from scientific findings.
- Revise conclusions when new evidence justifies doing so.

The project has an AI Canon and five methodological protocols. Their detailed public presentation will follow verification of the authoritative versions and the scope approved for publication.
