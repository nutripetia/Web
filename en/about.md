# About Nutripetia

Nutripetia is an independent initiative focused on **research, analysis, auditing and scientific communication about animal nutrition and welfare**, particularly dogs and cats.

Our purpose is to investigate before reaching conclusions, assess evidence consistently, acknowledge uncertainty and communicate findings without allowing commercial interests to dictate scientific outcomes.

We distinguish observations, hypotheses, interpretations and unknowns. We also distinguish scientific research from the evaluation of our own research processes.

Nutripetia is under development; research will be made public only after the required review and authorisation stages.
